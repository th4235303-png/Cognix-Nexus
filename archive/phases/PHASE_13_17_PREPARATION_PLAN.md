## Phase 13 — AI Active Layer
Feature slices:
- AI agent mode: overnight/cross-book analysis, contradictions, stale notes, progress and morning digest.
- Synthesis engine: multi-book frameworks with citations and version tracking.
- Decision support: evidence-backed perspectives with source citation.
- Writing assistant: outlines, library citations, drafts and style matching.
- Feynman mode: explain/re-explain loop with an explicit mastery threshold.

Existing agent_jobs, agent_runs and agent_findings remain the durable baseline. Before runtime activation, harden owner scope, auditable lifecycle events, lease/heartbeat recovery, idempotency/terminal fencing and evidence references across retries.

## Phase 14 — Learning Science
- deterministic knowledge-decay prediction and review reminders
- interleaving across subjects
- library-backed learning-path generation
- knowledge-gap analysis and source recommendations
- research mode with timelines, figures, contradictions and reports

Generated learning state remains derived, testable and evidence-linked.

## Phase 15 — Life Integration
- opt-in personal knowledge/life timeline
- local-only mood-aware recommendations
- user-triggered ambient audio/voice learning
- explicit context restoration
- encrypted time capsules with delayed unlock policy

Privacy, consent and recovery evidence are prerequisites.
